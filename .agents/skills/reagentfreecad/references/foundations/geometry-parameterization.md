# Geometry and parameters

Separate parameter domains from topological trimming. At seams or singularities, check periodicity and trimmed intervals; one edge can have different 2D parameter curves on adjacent faces.

Determine outward material normals from topological orientation and material classification, not surface orientation alone. Distinguish positional, tangent and curvature continuity; tangency supports a blend hypothesis without identifying its command.

Compare dimensions and directions in a declared frame with separate linear and angular tolerances. Use coaxiality, offsets and adjacency rather than entity numbering.
