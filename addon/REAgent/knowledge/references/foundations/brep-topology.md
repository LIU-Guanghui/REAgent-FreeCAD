# B-Rep topology

A carrier surface differs from its trimmed face. Use boundary loops, oriented adjacency and material side to infer feature relationships.

- A closed wire need not be planar or free of self-intersection.
- A closed shell need not form a manifold solid.
- A valid compound need not contain one connected solid.
- Coincident coordinates need not share topology.
- Matching carrier equations or topology counts need not describe the same bounded shape.

Inspect seams, degeneracies and inner loops when they affect the proposed operation.
