# Drawing to CAD

Observe the fully dimensioned DXF drawing in Source. Inspect the whole sheet first to identify the part, drawing units, projection, sections and detail views. Locate small callouts in that sheet and enlarge them until the text and the geometry it refers to are readable together. Use those callouts and clarify missing, illegible or conflicting dimensions that materially change the part.

Build one editable solid from the drawing's dimensions. Before delivery, cite a current Target view and the corresponding drawing view or section to check the profile, main features and section-defined geometry. For each key dimension, cite the drawing view and callout, then identify the corresponding Target feature or span. Use directly available Target measurements and express the nominal and applicable drawing tolerance in the observed unit. Explain dimensions derived from multiple observations and state discrepancies and unresolved items.
